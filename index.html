About the College

Moodlakatte Institute of Technology

Moodlakatte Institute of Technology was established in the year 2004. The college is situated near Kundapura, Udupi, Karnataka-576217 which is run by the Moodlakatte Nagarathna Bhujanga Shetty Trust (MNBS Trust).

The college is approved by the All-India Council of Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi and is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University Belagavi. The College is located at a walkable distance from Kundapura railway station.

Our institution is accredited by NAAC. The institution offers 6 UG programmes, namely CSE, ISE, ECE, CSE-DS, AI & ML, and CSE-AI & ML, and 2 PG programmes (MBA and MCA).

Departments

Information Science and Engineering

The Department of Information Science and Engineering (ISE) at Moodlakatte Institute of Technology was established in 2023 with an intake of 60 students. The department offers a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programme affiliated to VTU, Belagavi.

Computer Science Engineering Data Science

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) was established in 2022 and offers a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) with an intake of 60 students.
